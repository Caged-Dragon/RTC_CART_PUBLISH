# SEO keyword map — RedThunder Crackers

This plan is built around the live 2026 catalogue and the two canonical hostnames. It is deliberately written for product information/catalogue searches; it does not promise or imply online fireworks checkout. Search position cannot be guaranteed. Use natural phrasing in visible copy, titles, headings, descriptive internal links and image alt text; do not repeat keywords unnaturally. Google may rewrite snippets and title links based on the result and page content.

## Claims intentionally not targeted as online transactions

Phrases like “buy crackers online”, “online crackers booking”, “cash on delivery crackers online”, “place online order” and “online fireworks sale” are not used as purchase calls-to-action because this version is catalogue/enquiry only while legal compliance is reviewed. The business may separately confirm what descriptions are lawful and accurate. Do not add product ratings, stock availability, delivery guarantees, customer reviews or price claims unless they come from verified data.

## Technical verification checklist

- One unique, relevant title and meta description per indexable public page.
- Canonical URL points to its own preferred HTTPS URL.
- Sitemap lists only indexable, public catalogue/information URLs; account, cart, order history and tracking screens are excluded.
- Robots rules do not attempt to replace authentication or access control.
- Product names/prices come from `products` and current `product_prices`; images from `product_images`; categories from `product_categories`.
- Search Console: verify both domain properties, submit each sitemap, inspect canonical selection and fix crawl/indexing errors after deployment.
- Recheck titles, description lengths, mobile rendering, page speed, links and structured data after every release.
